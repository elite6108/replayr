Instant Replay

Capture instantly.

Replayr keeps a rolling buffer of your gameplay, so the last few minutes are always ready. One hotkey saves the moment without interrupting your game.

  • Always-on buffer while you play
  • Game and mic audio
  • Preview before saving
  • Game detection for titles you actually run

Edit

Make it yours.

Trim, crop, add your webcam, adjust audio, and export. The original file stays unchanged — Studio writes a new local MP4.

  • Timeline trim next to clip details
  • Webcam overlays in the scene
  • Crop for vertical posts
  • Export or share when the take is ready

Organize

Keep everything in one place.

Your clips live in a local library first. Keep them on this PC or in the cloud, organize with folders, and find a take without waiting on an upload.

  • Grid of clips on this machine
  • Titles, games, folders, and favorites
  • Local and cloud copies stay distinct
  • Delete local or cloud independently

Discover

A home for your clips.

Replayr.tv is for library, shares, and finding other players — not a replacement for desktop capture. Open a link from your phone. No desktop app required to watch.

  • Private, unlisted, or public when you send a link
  • Follow the people you actually watch
  • Public clips for later discovery
  • Quiet shares until you choose to send them

Frequently asked questions

What is Replayr?

Replayr is a gameplay clipper: Instant Replay and Record on the desktop, a local library, optional cloud copies, and share links that stay quiet until you send them.

What platforms will be supported?

Desktop app first, with web and mobile for library and shares. Capture stays on the desktop; phones are for watching and sending links.

Is there a mobile app?

Mobile is for the library and shares, not for replacing desktop capture. We’ll say more when those builds are ready to invite people into.

Do clips have to go to the cloud?

No. Files stay on this PC until you upload. Cloud copies are optional, and you can keep local and cloud versions separate.

When does it launch?

When beta opens. We don’t have a public date yet — waitlist mail is how you’ll hear.

What do I get for joining the waitlist?

Early access when we invite, launch mail, a beta invite, and a first look. We only email when there’s something to send.
